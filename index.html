About the journal

Research with context

Independent, student-led, and focused on learning through analysis.

KIRR (Kazakhstan International Relations Research) is an independent, student-led research initiative exploring international relations, global politics, diplomacy, international conflicts, and Kazakhstan’s role in the international system. The project aims to develop research, analytical thinking, and academic writing through clear questions, reliable sources, and balanced perspectives.

01 Evidence-based research
02 Multiple perspectives
03 Clear distinction between facts and analysis
04 Transparent references
Sources & methodology

Every claim needs a source

Articles will include references and explain how information was gathered. KIRR is an educational initiative and does not represent a government or international organization.
